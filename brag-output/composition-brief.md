## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Quiet gallery-grade product film for an elite chess academy"
- Interpretation: Obsidian velvet canvas (`#000000`), huge elegant serif type, charcoal elevation (`#1d1d1d`), ash hairlines (`#d6d5d0`), crisp transitions, zero gimmicks.
- Angle: Modern 1:1 mentorship over generic algorithms and pre-recorded videos.
- Hook: 0-3s high-contrast serif typography revealing the academy's philosophy.
- Outro / punchline: 14-18s clean brand lockup with URL and direct invitation to book.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS blue cards or colorful gradients
  - Comic-sans or cartoonish chess pieces
  - Hyperactive strobe effects
